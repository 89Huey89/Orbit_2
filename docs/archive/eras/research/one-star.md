# One star, eight names — research file

The table behind JOURNEY.md §9.2's later rounds (settled 2026-10-01): every round of the Journey after the
first follows one real star through all eight centuries — round 2 the Pleiades, round 3 Sirius, round 4
Antares, round 5 Aldebaran, then the four again. The game's copy of this table is `ONE_STARS` in
`src/onestar.js`; this file is where each name is argued for. Labels are ERA-AUDIT.md's three:
**attested**, **plausible reconstruction**, **gameplay translation**. A name nobody can attest is never
supplied: the entry says that none survives, and the label then records what the game itself is doing
(standing a body in for the star).

Compiled 2026-10-01. Wikipedia and many primary-source sites are blocked from the sandbox this was
written in; what was checked, and through what, is listed under each star and in "What could not be
verified" at the end.

## How the names are set

- **I · The Rock** has no script. No name for any of the four survives; the line says so in English
  (`THE PLEIADES · NO NAME SURVIVES`) and the note hedges as the wall's own chart notes do.
- **II · The Ceiling** names only what the decan lists and the Senenmut ceiling securely give. Names are
  spelt in signs from the Ceiling's own checked sign set (`CEILING_G`) under the body, and in Latin
  letters in the inscription; the Egyptological ꜣ is avoided because no embedded face carries it.
- **III · The Scroll** gives the lodge (xiu) or star name in kaishu with pinyin, without tone marks, as
  the Scroll's charts already do.
- **IV · The Astrolabe** gives the Arabic in naskh with a plain transliteration, as `ASTRO_STARS` does.
- **V · The Atlas** gives Bayer's letter (Uranometria, 1603).
- **VI · The Lens** gives the Flamsteed number — numbers assigned from Flamsteed's telescopic catalogue
  (1725; the numbers themselves were added in Lalande's edition, 1783).
- **VII · The Flyby** gives the Hipparcos number (ESA's astrometry satellite, 1989–93).
- **VIII · The Probe** sets the star in its own log format, name and distance, as `PRB_SYSTEMS` does. The
  designations and figures are real; the probe's log is the game's, so every entry is a gameplay
  translation.

## The Pleiades (round 2)

| Century | On the sheet | Label | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | THE PLEIADES · NO NAME SURVIVES | plausible reconstruction | Rappenglück (after Antequera Congregado 1992) reads six dots over a Lascaux bull's shoulder as the Pleiades; contested (Bahn; Hayden & Villeneuve, *CAJ* 21(3), 2011). research/rock.md §1, §12. |
| II | KHAU · THE PLEIADES? (signs ḫ-ꜣ-w + star) | plausible reconstruction | The decan ḫꜣw, "the Thousands", is most often read as the Pleiades — a majority reading, not a certainty (Sparavigna, arXiv:0810.1592; *Identifying the Ancient Egyptian Constellations*, Sino-Platonic Papers 253). The phonetic spelling with the star determinative is the game's, from its checked signs. |
| III | 昴 MAO · THE PLEIADES | attested | 昴, the eighteenth of the twenty-eight lodges. Shijing, Shao Nan, 小星: 嘒彼小星，維參與昴 ("twinkling small stars, Shen and Mao"). |
| IV | الثريا · AL-THURAYYA · THE PLEIADES | attested | al-thurayyā, a diminutive read as "the little abundant one", tied in the anwāʾ tradition to the rains (Kunitzsch; University of Arizona, *Arab Star Calendars*). |
| V | PLEIADES · η TAURI | attested | Bayer, *Uranometria* (1603): η Tauri = Alcyone, the brightest of the cluster. |
| VI | 25 TAURI · THE PLEIADES | attested | Flamsteed 25 Tau = Alcyone; Messier entered the cluster as M 45 on 4 March 1769, giving Alcyone's place (SEDS Messier pages). |
| VII | HIP 17702 · THE PLEIADES | attested | HIP 17702 = η Tau. Hipparcos put the cluster at 118–122 pc against 133–136 pc from other methods; VLBI settled 136.2 pc (Melis et al., *Science* 345, 2014; arXiv:1408.6544). |
| VIII | PLEIADES · 444 LY | gameplay translation | 136.2 pc ≈ 444 ly; age c. 100–125 Myr. "Logged; not a target" is the probe's. |

## Sirius (round 3)

| Century | On the sheet | Label | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | SIRIUS · NO NAME SURVIVES | gameplay translation | No Palaeolithic mark is read as Sirius, securely or otherwise; research/rock.md. |
| II | SOPDET · SIRIUS (signs s-p-d-t + star) | attested | Sopdet (Greek Sothis), seated behind Sah on the Senenmut ceiling, TT353; research/ceiling.md. Her heliacal rising opened the year. The usual spelling opens with the thorn sign (M44); the game spells it phonetically from its checked signs. |
| III | 天狼 TIANLANG · SIRIUS | attested | 天狼, the Celestial Wolf, in Sima Qian's *Tianguan shu* (Shiji, c. 100 BCE); the Bow and Arrow 弧矢 aims at it. |
| IV | الشعرى · AL-SHIRA · SIRIUS | attested | Qur'an 53:49, "He is the Lord of al-Shiʿrā"; al-shiʿrā al-yamāniya in the star lists (Kunitzsch). |
| V | SIRIUS · α CANIS MAIORIS | attested | Bayer, *Uranometria* (1603). |
| VI | 9 CANIS MAIORIS · SIRIUS | attested | Flamsteed 9 CMa. Bessel announced an unseen companion from Sirius's wobble in 1844; Alvan Graham Clark saw it on 31 January 1862 while testing the 18½-inch Dearborn refractor (AMNH; Astronomy.com). |
| VII | HIP 32349 · SIRIUS | attested | HIP 32349 = α CMa. Voyager 2 will pass within 4.3 ly of Sirius in about 296,000 years (NASA/JPL Voyager pages, as repeated by several secondary sources). |
| VIII | SIRIUS · 8.60 LY | gameplay translation | The Probe's own `PRB_SYSTEMS` entry; Sirius A c. 2 solar masses with a white-dwarf companion. |

## Antares (round 4)

| Century | On the sheet | Label | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | ANTARES · NO NAME SURVIVES | gameplay translation | No Palaeolithic mark is read as Antares. |
| II | ANTARES · NO NAME IS CERTAIN | gameplay translation | Decan identifications beyond Sah, Sopdet and Meskhetiu are uncertain (research/ceiling.md). | III | 大火 DAHUO · ANTARES | attested | 大火, the Great Fire, = 心宿二, the middle star of the lodge 心 (Xin, the Heart). Shijing, Bin Feng, 七月: 七月流火. |
| IV | قلب العقرب · QALB AL-AQRAB · ANTARES | attested | qalb al-ʿaqrab, the heart of the scorpion (al-Ṣūfī, 964; Kunitzsch), after Ptolemy's "middle of the three bright ones in the body". |
| V | ANTARES · α SCORPII | attested | Ἀντάρης in the Almagest, "rival of Ares" for its colour; Bayer's α. |
| VI | 21 SCORPII · ANTARES | attested | Flamsteed 21 Sco. Johann Tobias Bürg saw the companion in Vienna during the lunar occultation of 13 April 1819. |
| VII | HIP 80763 · ANTARES | attested | HIP 80763 = α Sco; Hipparcos parallax 5.89 ± 1.00 mas (c. 170 pc, c. 550 ly); radius c. 680 solar. |
| VIII | ANTARES · 550 LY | gameplay translation | A red supergiant and core-collapse supernova progenitor (*RNAAS*, 2025, doi:10.3847/2515-5172/adb3a1). |

## What could not be verified

- No Wikipedia page could be opened; facts above were checked through search results quoting it and
  through the other sources named. The Flamsteed and Hipparcos numbers (25 Tau / HIP 17702, 9 CMa /
  HIP 32349, 21 Sco / HIP 80763, 87 Tau / HIP 21421) were each confirmed in at least one search result.
- Kunitzsch was not read directly; the Arabic names are the standard ones and agree with the
  Astrolabe's own table, which an earlier pass checked against him.
- The Khau = Pleiades reading rests on secondary summaries (Sparavigna; Sino-Platonic Papers 253); the
  primary decan literature (Neugebauer & Parker; Lull & Belmonte) was not opened. It is labelled a
  plausible reconstruction and the line carries a question mark for that reason.
- The hieroglyphic spellings of Khau and Sopdet are the game's phonetic spellings from its checked sign
  set, not copied from a particular monument.
- The Voyager 2 / Sirius and Pioneer 10 / Aldebaran figures were seen in NASA-sourced secondary pages,
  not on the JPL page itself.
- "Oculus Tauri" and "Palilicium" for Aldebaran come from Allen (1899) as quoted; whether Bayer's own
  plate prints either was not checked, so the atlas's line sets only the Bayer letter.
